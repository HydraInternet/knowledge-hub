> [!QUESTION] What is this?
> HydraNet is an [incorporated association](https://www.acnc.gov.au/for-charities/start-charity/you-start-charity/charity-subtypes/incorporated-associations) in Australia. One requirement of becoming an association is to have a set of rules. These rules must follow [schedule 1](https://www.consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/inc-guide-incorporated-associations-western-australia/schedule-1-matters-be-provided-rules) and are instructed to do so by law. These rules are documented here for all members to access.
